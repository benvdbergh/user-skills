# Skill escalation — value-propositions

## Owns

- Selecting and combining **value proposition frameworks** (fit, jobs, value types, alternatives, proof discipline)
- Structuring **draft** value propositions, canvases, and critique memos
- Flagging **missing organizational inputs** and **unstated assumptions**
- Recommending **validation approaches** (hypotheses, experiments) at a methodological level

## Does not own

- **Primary research execution** (customer interviews, surveys, conjoint studies) — delegate planning to research workflows or human researchers; the skill may define what to learn
- **Pricing decisions**, **contracts**, **financial modeling** as authoritative outputs — economic framing is illustrative; numbers require business owners and data
- **Legal, regulatory, or compliance** approval of claims — flag “claim risk” only
- **Brand voice and corporate communications** final sign-off — coordinate with org-specific guides
- **Full go-to-market strategy** — this skill supports messaging layers; portfolio strategy belongs to product/strategy processes

## Suggested handoffs

| Adjacent need | Skill or owner |
|---------------|----------------|
| Segments, sizing, competitive landscape | `market-segmentation-research` (if deep market quant), or strategy/product owners |
| Product capabilities, features, roadmap truth | `product-knowledge-catalog`, PRDs, internal product docs |
| Specs, PRDs, technical plans from VP work | `specification` |
| Customer-facing technical docs from stable messaging | `tech-documentation` |
| Enterprise / ArchiMate modeling of capabilities | `enterprise-architecture` (global) + vault model store workflows |
| Product feature taxonomy: feasibility vs docs, gap placement in feature sheets, release-to-doc sync (Ai-Vault stackFleet feature model) | `feature-model-navigation` (Ai-Vault project skill) |

## Whole-product warning (Moore)

If the proposition implies an integrated solution, explicitly surface **dependencies** (integrations, services, data, organizational roles). A credible VP often requires a **whole product** story or scoped boundary—do not imply full coverage without naming what is in and out of scope.
