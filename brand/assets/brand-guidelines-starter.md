## 4. Brand Voice

### Brand Personality

| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Professional** | Credible and precise without being stiff |
| **Clear** | Plain language; short sentences |
| **Approachable** | Helpful tone; avoid jargon |

### Core Attributes

| Attribute | Guideline |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Clarity** | Lead with the outcome; one idea per sentence |
| **Consistency** | Use approved terms and color names |
| **Confidence** | Active voice; specific claims |

### Prohibited

| Avoid |
|-------|
| Hype without proof |
| Competitor bashing |
| All caps for emphasis |

### Forbidden Phrases

- "Revolutionary" without evidence
- "Best in class" without context
- "Synergy"

## 5. AI Image Generation
