# Industry Standards and Prior Art

Related: `../SKILL.md`, `framework-selection-by-scale.md`, `integration-boundaries.md`

This reference summarizes reusable standards and best practices used by this skill.

## Standards Snapshot

| Source | Practice used in this skill | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| SAFe Roadmap | Multi-horizon roadmaps: PI, product/solution, portfolio | Supports planning at different organizational scales |
| SAFe Architectural Runway | Balance emergent design with intentional architecture | Prevents feature delivery stalls caused by missing technical foundation |
| SAFe WSJF | Economic sequencing via Cost of Delay over Job Size | Helps select what should ship first under constraints |
| Roman Pichler (Outcome-based roadmap) | Goal-oriented roadmaps with metrics and collaborative alignment | Keeps roadmap tied to outcomes, not feature wishlists |
| SVPG roadmap alternative | Outcome and objective-led planning over feature mandates | Improves team autonomy and reduces delivery waste |
| Intercom RICE | Reach-impact-confidence-effort prioritization | Useful for product-level prioritization where WSJF is too portfolio-heavy |

## Notes for Use

- Prefer **outcome-led planning** when uncertainty is high and solution details are still fluid.
- Prefer **economic sequencing** (WSJF/RICE) when release slots are constrained and trade-offs are required.
- Always keep a visible **runway/enabler layer** in roadmap views for architecture sustainability.
