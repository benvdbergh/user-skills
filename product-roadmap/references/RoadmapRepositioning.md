# Workflow: RoadmapRepositioning

## Trigger

"Adjust roadmap due to new strategy, architecture shift, or market signal."

## Steps

1. Capture what changed:
   - strategy or outcome shift
   - architecture constraint/update
   - market or competitive signal
2. Identify impacted roadmap items by horizon and confidence level.
3. Re-score impacted items (WSJF/RICE/strategic score).
4. Re-sequence outcomes, features, and enablers.
5. Quantify impact:
   - release movement
   - dependency changes
   - confidence changes
   - risk increase/decrease
6. Produce stakeholder-ready impact summary using:
   - `assets/roadmap-repositioning-impact-template.md`

## Done Criteria

- Roadmap reflects latest assumptions and evidence.
- Delta from prior plan is explicit and explainable.
- Impact summary includes schedule confidence and risk implications.
